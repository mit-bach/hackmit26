# What is good

Do not “fix” these by deleting them. A later agent that collapses them will recreate V1.

---

## Grain split

Fifteen standing Bots (plus the disputed sixteenth, World) is the right kind of compression.

- Source format is not a Bot. EDI vs PDF vs portal is a Connector or a Profile on `email` / `books`.
- Reviewer twin is not a second Operator Bot. Concurrence is `ctl-*`.
- Close treatments share a month-end Wake. They are Profiles on `close` / `ctl-books`, not four extra lanes.
- Sample-data Display names are eval Kernel. They are not floor Bots.
- `pay` is not a Profile on `ap`. Match is “do we owe this.” Pay is “does cash leave this week.”
- `apply` is not a Profile on `collect`. Collections before application duns people who paid.

Tests A–D are good law. Keep them.

## Verifier idea

Three Verifiers by stake class, not twelve reviewer clones, not one human Operator.

SoD as separate slugs and Grant denylist is the right shape. A Verifier that cannot hold the Operator Bot’s write Grants is good.

`audit` as assurance, not a fourth Verifier on Friday’s pay run, is good.

## Handle protocol (when it is actually used)

Accept ≠ complete. Sender never marks complete. `blocked` is not done. Rooms 2–6. Per-Bot Memory isolation. Routines fire onto the owning Bot, not into whoever is at the keyboard.

On 2026-09-20 the AP stub path proved Handle complete on live Pi for `email` → `ap` → `ctl-pay`. The Bots HOLDed and REFUSEd a marker file instead of inventing INV-S12. That refusal is good behavior on bad evidence.
